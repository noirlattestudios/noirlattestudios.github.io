Sootloam Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information is handled when you play Sootloam (the "game"), a puzzle game for Android published on Google Play by Noirlatte Studios ("we", "us"). Noirlatte Studios is the name under which Franck Thiombiano, an individual developer, publishes games. We are the data controller for this policy.

Questions? Email noirlattestudios@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, or other contact details inside the game.
  • Your progress, coins, boosters, and stall pieces are saved only on your device. We don't run our own servers, and we can't see your save data.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and usage information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before using your data for personalized ads.
  • Optional purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment, and we never see your card or payment details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following data locally on your phone so it can remember where you are:

  • level progress and level completion
  • coins, boosters (Tongs, Spare, Stir), and unlocked market-stall pieces
  • settings such as sound and music preferences
  • which purchases you own (for example, whether ads have been removed), so the game can apply them
  • your ad consent choice, if you were shown a consent message (stored by Google's consent tool)

This data stays in the game's private storage on your device. It isn't sent to us, and we have no way to access it. If your device's Android backup is turned on, Android may include the game's data in your device backup to your Google Account. That backup is handled by Google under your Google Account settings, and we can't access it either.

Deleting it: uninstall the game, or go to Settings > Apps > Sootloam > Storage > Clear storage (the exact menu names vary by device). This permanently erases your progress. Purchases you made through Google Play can be restored afterward (see section 3).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free to play and supported by ads: optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward), full-screen ads shown between levels later in the game, and banner ads on menu screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland).

When ads are requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game automatically collects and sends to Google information such as:

  • Device and account identifiers: your device's Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (for example, your city or country)
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and screen size
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, ad views, taps, and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app and ad performance, launch time, and hang rate

Google uses this information to show ads, measure how ads perform, limit how often you see the same ad, and detect and prevent fraud and abuse. If ad personalization is allowed, Google may also use it to show ads based on your interests. According to Google, this data is encrypted in transit.

The information goes directly from your device to Google. We don't receive it in a form that identifies you. We only see aggregated reports, such as how many ads were shown.

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices and consent

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: when you first open the game, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks whether Google and its ad partners may use your data for personalized ads and related purposes. You can accept, decline, or manage your choices. Declining doesn't stop the game from working; you may still see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy options button in the game's settings.
  • Everywhere:
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on most Android devices, open Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices, Settings > Google > Ads). Deleting the advertising ID stops apps from using it for ads.
  • Turn off personalized ads from Google: visit My Ad Center.
  • Remove ads in the game: an optional purchase removes banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads, coin packs, and a starter pack. All purchases are processed by Google Play using Google Play Billing.

  • Google collects and processes your payment information under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • We never see or store your credit card number or other payment details.
  • Google tells the game which items you have bought, so the game can deliver them and restore them if you reinstall (use Restore purchases in the shop). This purchase information is used only to deliver what you bought.
  • Google Play gives us limited transaction records in our developer console (for example, an order number, the item bought, the price, the date, and the country), which we use only for accounting, refunds, and customer support. Google decides what order information we can see, and it never includes your full payment card details.

For refunds, see Google Play's refund options at support.google.com/googleplay, or email us.

4. Information we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, or precise location. The game has no account system, no chat, and no social features. We don't add any separate analytics or tracking tools beyond what the Google services described above include.

If you email us, we'll receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete support emails when they're no longer needed.

5. Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own purposes. The only third party that receives data from the game is Google, through AdMob and Google Play Billing, as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Data retention

  • Save data stays on your device until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it.
  • Advertising and purchase data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. See How Google retains data we collect.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

7. Your rights and how to delete data or opt out

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EEA, the UK, or certain US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain processing.

Because we don't hold personal data about you on our own systems, most requests are handled through these controls:

What you want How to do it
Delete your game progress Clear the game's storage or uninstall it (section 1)
Change or withdraw ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland) Privacy options in the game's settings
Stop personalized ads Delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or use My Ad Center
See ads less Buy the optional remove-ads item
Data Google holds about you Use your Google Account tools at myaccount.google.com or Google's privacy controls
Anything else Email noirlattestudios@gmail.com; we'll reply within 30 days

If you're in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases (EEA/UK): where the GDPR applies, data used for personalized advertising, and storing or accessing information on your device for ads, relies on the consent you give in the consent message. Google explains the legal grounds it relies on for its own processing, such as preventing fraud and measuring ads, in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

Sootloam is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The game doesn't ask for age or personal details. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, for example by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

The game doesn't send your save data anywhere. Data exchanged with Google's ad and billing services is sent over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we've kept data handling to a minimum.

10. International users

Google may process advertising and purchase data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under its own safeguards as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because the game adds a new feature or service, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If the change is significant, we'll also mention it in the game's update notes.
